- **The money keypad has a `000` key, and the cash fields add a note with one tap.** Where the
  store's currency has no minor unit (đồng, yen), the keypad adds `000`, so 150,000₫ is three taps
  rather than six. On the shift's float, cash paid in or out, and the count, the store's four
  largest published notes sit above the digits, each adding one note to the amount typed
  (`docs/ui-ux.md` §2). English and Vietnamese.
