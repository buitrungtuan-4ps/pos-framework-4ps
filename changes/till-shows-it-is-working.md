- **A tap that writes says so at once, and a double tap sends one request.** **Send**, the cash,
  card and QR tenders, and every act on the till's own drawer (opening the shift, the count, the
  close and its reason, cash paid in or out, and the drawer opened without a sale) read *Sending…*
  or *Taking payment…* from the frame of the tap, and take no second tap until the edge answers.
  The buttons used to look untouched while the request was out, so a second tap sent a second
  settle, send or drawer act behind the first (`docs/ui-ux.md` §4).
