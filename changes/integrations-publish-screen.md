- **The console publishes a store's integrations** (phase 10 open item O5). The Integrations screen
  has a **Publish integrations** card for the store in context: it sends the connections that serve
  the store and the delivery apps it takes orders from, shows when they were last published, and
  says what the store now carries. Before, a connection or a delivery app's settings changed in the
  console reached no store without a direct API call.
