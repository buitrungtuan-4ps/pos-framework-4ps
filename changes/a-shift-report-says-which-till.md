- **The printed shift report says which till, when, how many bills by which tender, and why.**
  Under its heading it names the till, then when the shift opened and closed in the store's time,
  how many bills it settled and what cash, card, QR and other tenders took, and after the verdict
  the reason the close gave for an over or short. It names no person. A slip for several drawers
  prints the same for each.
