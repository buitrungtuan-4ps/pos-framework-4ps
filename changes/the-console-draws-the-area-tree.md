- **The Floor screen shows the area tree.** An area can be added inside another, and a branch moves
  by dragging it onto another area or onto *Top level*, or from its *Move to* list. The plan heads
  each grid with its breadcrumb, the cloud's refusals show on the row they are about, and a plan
  deeper than three levels is a warning, never a refusal (ADR-0211).
