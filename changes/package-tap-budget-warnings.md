- **A package version's taps are counted from its homes, and an act over its budget is a warning**
  (ADR-0210). The version still publishes.
  - For each role and device class, a common act more than two taps from the home, or a rare one
    more than three, is listed by the dry run with where it is and what it costs.
  - The screen designer notes each beside the node that offers it, and the check bar says how many.
  - A published version keeps its count of warnings, and comparing two versions shows both counts.
