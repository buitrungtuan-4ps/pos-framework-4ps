- **A bar tab asks eat in or take away where the store asks each walk-in guest.** On a store whose
  walk-in channel is "ask", **Tab → Mở tab** opened every tab as takeaway, so a tab drunk at the bar
  was priced from the takeaway book and taxed as takeaway. **Mở tab** now offers **Ăn tại chỗ** and
  **Mang về**, as the counter's **Đơn mới** does, and opens the tab on the answer.
