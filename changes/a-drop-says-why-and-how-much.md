- **A drop to the safe says why, and the shift says how much it dropped**
  ([ADR-0186](../docs/adr/0186-a-drawer-is-counted-by-denomination.md) amendment). After the pile to
  drop, the Shift screen offers the store's reasons for a drop (*Nộp két* by default): one tap on a
  reason records the drop with it, and **Drop … to the safe** records it with none. Under paid in
  and paid out, the open shift shows what it has dropped to the safe so far, and the close card
  lists it beside them.
