- **HQ designs the till's screens by drag and drop.** The package designer's *Screens* tab adds
  screens and a layout per device class, and builds each from the release's components: drag one
  from the palette onto the screen, or add and move it from the keyboard, and set its labels,
  choices and acts in the inspector. A refusal from the cloud outlines the part it names.
