- **A discount is no longer lost when a bill is split.** After a manager-approved discount, splitting
  the bill by item opened parts that owed their lines in full, so the guests were asked for the
  undiscounted total. Each part now takes a share of the bill's discount and comp in proportion to
  what its lines come to, the remainder on the last part, so the parts' reductions add up to the
  bill's exactly; a restart rebuilds the same shares from the log. Merging bills keeps what was
  taken off each. No event, route or setting changes. (pilot run 2, N3)
