- **A reprinted receipt now prints the e-invoice number and lookup code** (`Số HĐĐT`, `Mã tra cứu`)
  that the original receipt printed (ADR-0242 decision 3). Before, a copy from **Hôm nay → In lại**
  left them out, so a guest who asked for a second copy had no way to look the invoice up. A copy of
  a bill whose deferred invoice has since been issued prints its number; one still waiting prints
  none.
