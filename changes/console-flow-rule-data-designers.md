- **HQ designs a package's flows, reactions and custom data in the console.** The package designer's
  Flows tab draws each flow as a graph of steps, required or optional, conditional, waiting on the
  steps before them; the rule builder writes every condition as CEL from choices, for steps and for
  "when … if … then …" reactions that never move money; the Data tab declares custom fields, lists
  and object types, and asks of every field whether it is personal data, with its retention and
  consent purpose. Refusals show beside the flow, step or reaction they name.
