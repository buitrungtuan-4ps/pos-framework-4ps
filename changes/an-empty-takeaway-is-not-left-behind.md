- **A counter order is opened by its first item.** **New order** goes straight to the order screen
  and the order is opened on the edge, with its queue number, when the first item is added, so a
  guest who leaves before ordering leaves no empty order behind. Such an order used to take a queue
  number and could never be listed or closed (`docs/ui-ux.md` §3).
