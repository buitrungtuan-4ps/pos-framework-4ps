- **The till weighs an item sold by weight** (ADR-0223). Tapping, scanning or keying a weighed
  item opens the weight display: it shows what the scale beside the till reads and adds the item at
  its settled weight, priced per kilogram by the store server; where the store takes a typed weight,
  a keypad takes the grams. The bakery-retail template's demo menu sells a loaf by weight.
