- **A till no longer keeps every dish of the day in memory.** The lines of an order are forgotten
  once it is paid, closed owing nothing, or voided after a part was paid, and each read of the open
  orders forgets the lines of any order it no longer lists. A counter order paid before it is
  cooked stays on the kitchen board until the kitchen is done with it, as before
  (`docs/ui-ux.md` §4).
