- **A print says it is printing at once, and a print with no answer is said not confirmed.** The
  pre-bill, a copy of a receipt, the X and a handover slip again read *Sending…* on the tap and take
  no second tap until the store server answers; a refusal says why. When no answer comes the till
  says the print is not confirmed and to check the printer before printing again, and never sends
  it again itself. A print never holds **Take payment**: a slow or silent printer no longer keeps a
  guest waiting at the till.
