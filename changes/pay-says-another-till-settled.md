- **The pay screen says when another till paid or voided its bill.** The tenders give way to
  *This bill was paid on another till* or *This bill was voided on another till* as soon as the
  event arrives, with the way back: the floor for a paid bill, the order for a voided one. The
  screen used to wait for the next tap to fail against a closed bill (`docs/ui-ux.md` §3).
  English and Vietnamese.
