- **A counter bill takes a discount and can be voided, as a table's can.** The counter's pay pad
  offers the table pay screen's own **Discount** and **Void this bill** panels, with the same
  reasons and manager's badge and PIN. A voided counter bill goes back to the list, which says no
  money was taken, and the order can be charged again. Splitting stays a table's act
  (`docs/ui-ux.md` §3).
