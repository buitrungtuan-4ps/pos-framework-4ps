- **Confirming or refusing a guest's QR order, and bumping a ticket, show at once.** The order leaves
  the guest queue, and the ticket the kitchen board or the pass, on the tap, before the store server
  answers. A refusal brings it back with its sentence; with no answer the till brings it back and
  reads the queue or the open orders again, since the store server may have made it, and never
  sends it again. A bump the store server refused used to fail silently and leave the ticket on the
  board with no word why; the board and the pass now say why.
